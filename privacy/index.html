WONDERWIRE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Wonderwire is a social media page with short, useful posts about technology, AI and science. To prepare and publish its own posts, the Wonderwire team uses a private tool (“the app”). This policy explains what the app does with data. The app is not offered to the public: only the Wonderwire team signs in to it.

What we access

When a Wonderwire team member connects Wonderwire's own TikTok account (with TikTok's official login), the app receives:

  • basic profile information of that account (its TikTok ID, display name and profile picture), to show which account is connected;
  • permission to upload Wonderwire's photo posts to that account's drafts, where a person reviews them and publishes them in the TikTok app.

We do not read anyone else's data, messages, contacts or followers, and we do not collect data about people who view our posts.

How we use it

Only to send Wonderwire's own posts to Wonderwire's own account. Nothing is posted without a team member's approval in the app.

Where it is kept

  • Access tokens are kept on our server and in our database (Supabase), never in a browser and never shared.
  • The pictures of a post are kept privately, and the cloud copies are deleted 7 days after the post has been published on every platform.
  • When a post is sent to the TikTok drafts, its slide pictures are reachable for a few minutes through long, unguessable links, so TikTok can download them; they are deleted as soon as TikTok has them.

Who else is involved

We do not sell or rent data. The app runs on service providers that only process data to run it: Render (hosting), GitHub Pages (these public pages), Supabase (database and file storage) and Google Gemini (writing post texts). They do not receive TikTok account data beyond what storing and running the app needs.

Disconnecting and deletion

The connection can be removed at any time in the app, or in TikTok under Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. When it is removed, the stored tokens are deleted. To ask us to delete any data, write to us through the contact details on the Wonderwire profiles on Instagram, TikTok or Facebook.

Children

Wonderwire's posts are made for a general adult audience, and the app is not meant for or used by children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date.

Contact

Write to us through the contact details on the Wonderwire profiles on Instagram, TikTok or Facebook.
